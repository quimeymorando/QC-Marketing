## 3. The zones

### Surface · 0–10 m
**You paid for ads. You got clicks. You didn't sell a thing.**
> Four people message you one day, then nothing for a week. And nobody can make sense of the report you get.
> We build campaigns that bring in people who can and want to buy. Then we show you the numbers in plain words: what you spent, who wrote in, who bought.

`Instagram & Facebook ads`

### Sunlight zone · 10–200 m
**You paid for a website. A year later, not a single inquiry.**
> Or worse: whoever built it vanished, and you don't even have the passwords.
> We build websites that explain what you do in seconds, look right on a phone and get people to reach out. The domain, the logins and the passwords are yours from day one.

`Websites & apps`

### Twilight zone · 200–1,000 m
**Your leads go cold somewhere between WhatsApp and a spreadsheet.**
> You're chasing payments, bookings, the reminder you forgot to send. You tried a CRM and it became one more job.
> We connect the tools you already use so follow-up happens on its own. You know who wrote in, where it stands and who needs a nudge, without typing anything in by hand.

`Automation & CRM · GoHighLevel, Make, n8n`

### The abyss · 1,000 m and beyond
**They message you at 11 p.m. You reply at 10 a.m. They've already bought somewhere else.**
> Between one client and the next you can't keep up, and you can't be in two places at once.
> We train an assistant on how your business works. It answers on WhatsApp and Instagram at any hour, spots who's genuinely interested and books the meeting. When a conversation needs a human, it hands it to you.

`Applied AI`

## 4. Before we go deeper

**Got charged by someone who then disappeared?**
You always talk to the two of us. No rotating account managers, no "we'll pass this on to the right team."

**Not sure what you're actually paying for?**
Before we start, you get two lines: what we'll do, what it costs and when it's ready.

**Tried an agency before and it didn't work?**
We start small, check that it works, and only then add the next piece.

**No time to learn another tool?**
You don't have to. We set it up, walk you through it once, and keep it running.

**Not sure AI fits your line of work?**
It might not. We'll tell you on the first call, before you spend a cent.
